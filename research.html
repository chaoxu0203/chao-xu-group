Research

Our group develops sustainable porous materials for energy and environmental applications. We focus on the design, green synthesis, and engineering of functional porous materials with tailored structures and properties.

Research Focus

Materials

  • Covalent Organic Frameworks (COFs)
  • Porous Organic Polymers (POPs)
  • Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs)

Applications

  • Gas separation
  • Carbon capture and conversion
  • Critical metal recovery
  • Nanofiltration
  • Energy harvesting and storage
