Welcome

Our research focuses on the green synthesis and engineering of porous materials, including metal-organic frameworks (MOFs), covalent organic frameworks (COFs), and porous organic polymers (POPs), for energy and environmental applications.

Research Focus

  • Porous Organic Polymers (POPs)
  • Covalent Organic Frameworks (COFs)
  • Carbon capture and conversion
  • Functional materials for water purification
